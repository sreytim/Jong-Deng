About Jong Deng

Connecting youths with opportunities that transform lives

Our Mission

At Jong Deng, our mission is to democratize access to life-changing opportunities for students and young professionals. We believe that talent is universal, but opportunity is not.

By creating a centralized platform that connects ambitious individuals with scholarships, internships, volunteer programs, exchange opportunities, and workshops, we aim to break down barriers and empower the next generations.

Equal Access
Global Reach
Empowerment

Our Vision

We aim to build a platform where young people can effortlessly discover trusted opportunities that help them grow, learn, and explore their potential.

Our vision is to make Jong Deng a dependable guide for youth seeking meaningful experiences that strengthen their skills, broaden their perspectives, and support their future ambitions.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Accessibility

Making opportunities available to everyone, everywhere, regardless of their circumstances or background.

Integrity

Maintaining transparency and honesty in all our partnerships and communications with users.

Excellence

Curating only the highest quality opportunities that truly make a meaningful difference.
