Frequently Asked Questions 💡

How quickly will I receive a response?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within **24-48 hours** during business days. For urgent matters, please call our phone number directly for immediate assistance.

Can I submit opportunities to be listed?

Yes! If you represent an organization offering scholarships, internships, or other opportunities, please **contact us with the details** using the form above. We are happy to review your submission promptly.

Do you offer application assistance?

While we don't directly assist with applications, we provide **comprehensive resources and guidance** to help you submit strong applications. Contact us for more information about our support services.
